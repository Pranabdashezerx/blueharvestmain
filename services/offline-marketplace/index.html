02

After the price

Pack, hold, dispatch

The lot does not leave the building as a loose crate. Packing, cold hold, and the truck are part of the same desk.

Pack

  • Lots leave in a standard pack
  • Not a one-off wrap
  • Ready for the buyer’s receiving dock

Hold

  • Cold storage on the same site
  • For catch that is not moving today
  • Temperature kept with the lot

Dispatch

  • Logistics coordinated from the hub
  • From the floor to the buyer
  • One handoff, not a second market
